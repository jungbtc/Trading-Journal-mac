TRADING JOURNAL FOR MAC

INSTALL
1. Open the DMG.
2. Drag Trading Journal into Applications.
3. Open Trading Journal from Applications.
4. Choose a journal password, then use the setup wizard to add wallets and
   read-only exchange/API keys. The setup code is filled in automatically.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 12 Monterey or later.
Python is included. No Terminal, Node, pip, or developer tools are needed.
An internet connection and your own provider keys are needed to sync live data.
The optional upstream AI features require a separately installed Claude CLI.

YOUR DATA
The journal, settings and keys stay on this Mac in:
~/Library/Application Support/Trading Journal/
Use Trading Journal > Open Data Folder to see it without Terminal.
Replacing the app does not delete this folder. Deleting the app leaves it intact.
The app only serves its dashboard on this computer (127.0.0.1).
Quit the app to stop syncing. Leave it open while importing historical records.
Closing the last window quits the app. It can take a few seconds to stop safely.

If you forget the password, choose Trading Journal > Reset Login Password.
This resets the login only; your journal and provider keys stay saved.
If startup fails, choose Trading Journal > Open Logs for diagnostic files.
Keep backups of your data folder. Do not share this folder or its credentials.

SHARING
Send the DMG file to another person. It contains the app and bundled runtimes,
with no personal wallets, journal, passwords, or API keys.
Each person sets up their own journal after installing it.
